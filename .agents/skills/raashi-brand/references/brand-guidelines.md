# Raashi Cognitive Technologies — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence & Persona
Raashi Cognitive Technologies Pvt. Ltd. represents the cutting edge of enterprise AI and knowledge synthesis. 
- **Voice**: Authoritative, intelligent, precise, clear, and reassuring.
- **Visual Personality**: High-end enterprise SaaS aesthetic inspired by Linear, Stripe, and Apple. Clean white canvas, strong dark navy structure, crisp electric blue interactive controls, with vivid cognitive gradients for hero sections.

## Mission Statement
*"Transforming Knowledge into Intelligent Solutions"*

## Visual Pillars
1. **Structural Clarity**: Interfaces use generous whitespace and 8-point spatial grid alignment.
2. **Cognitive Contrast**: Deep dark navy text (`#17285E`) on clean slate surface (`#F8FAFC`) or crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`).
3. **Controlled Accentuation**: Primary Blue (`#0560DF`) directs user intent; Orange (`#F94F0E`) highlights critical callouts; Red (`#EE3128`) signals destruction or high alert.

## Core Architectural Archetypes
- **Marketing / Landing**: Wide containers, bold cognitive gradient hero headings, feature grids with subtle shadows and 16px radius cards.
- **Enterprise SaaS Dashboard**: Dark Navy header/sidebar accents, ultra-clean surface grid cards, high-density data visualizations adhering strictly to brand palette tones.
- **Admin Panels & Control Planes**: Crisp 8px radius inputs, tabular data with clean contrast, visible focus states for keyboard navigation.
