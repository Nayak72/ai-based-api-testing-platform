# Project Guidelines & Design Standards

## Raashi Cognitive Technologies Brand & Design Skill Guidelines
Whenever building, designing, modifying, or refactoring any frontend user interfaces, pages, components, landing pages, or web/mobile applications in this codebase, you MUST strictly reference and follow the guidelines and design systems in:

1. **`raashi-brand`**: Located at [.agents/skills/raashi-brand/SKILL.md](.agents/skills/raashi-brand/SKILL.md)
   - Official Brand Identity & Design System for Raashi Cognitive Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Transforming Knowledge into Intelligent Solutions"). Enforces exact brand colors (`#0560DF`, `#0E2F9D`, `#17285E`, `#F94F0E`, `#EE3128`, `#D11753`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`), strict prohibitions (no green/yellow/teal/random pinks/purples), cognitive gradients, 8-point spatial grid, 8px/12px/16px/24px radii, and Lucide/shadcn standards.

2. **`design-taste-frontend`**: Located at [.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md](.agents/skills/design-taste-frontend/SKILL.md)
   - Follow brief inference, anti-slop guidelines, visual hierarchy, polished typography, real design systems, dynamic layouts, and pre-flight checks. Avoid generic templated designs.

3. **`ui-ux-pro-max`**: Located at [.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md](.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max/SKILL.md)
   - Leverage design intelligence, curated color palettes, font pairings, component patterns, micro-interactions, accessibility rules, and technology stack guidance.
